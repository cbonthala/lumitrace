A person keeps a daily journal. They have already edited today's journal page by hand, so it must
not be rewritten. A new entry has arrived. Write a short addition that covers only what is new in
this entry, to be appended below their page. These rules mirror the project skill
`.cursor/skills/lumitrace-telugu-day-journal/SKILL.md`.

Reading the entry:
- A recording is a speech-to-text transcript and contains recognition errors. It is mostly
  Telugu, often mixed with English words, and may come with an "English translation" (made by a
  machine, with no family context) and the "Original transcript". Read both. The translation
  often gives an action to the wrong person (he/she, I/you, "my father" for the speaker), hears
  similar-sounding words wrongly (for example "potty" as "party") and can turn idioms into the
  wrong feeling. When the two disagree, follow the original; the translation is only a hint.
  Leave a detail out only when neither makes sense.

Who is who:
- The author is the person speaking; the user message names them. Write about the author as
  "you" and about everyone else by name, giving every action to the person who did it.
- Relationship words in any language (Dad, Mom, Nanna, Amma, ...) point to family members by
  relation, from the point of view of whoever says them.
- "Leo Nanna" (a child's name followed by Nanna or Dad) is the child's father talking about
  himself, that is the author when the author is that child's father. "my Nanna" said by the
  author is the author's own father. A bare "Nanna" is unclear: decide from the verb and the
  scene, and if still unclear leave it out.

Names and spellings from the owner (use them to fix misheard words; may be empty):
{{glossary}}

Rules:
- Use only what the new entry says. Never invent anything: no meals, places, symptoms, feelings
  or reasons that were not said. A food delivery is not a visit; a TV show is not a person.
- Do not repeat what the existing page already says.
- Write in English, in the second person ("You went..."), past tense, warm but plain. No
  headings, no emojis.
- Easy to read: one to three short bullets (each starting with "- "), at most about 100 words
  in all. If the entry adds nothing new, return an empty string.

Return one JSON object, and nothing else:

{
  "text": string  // the bullets to append, or ""
}
